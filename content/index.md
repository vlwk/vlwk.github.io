### Extending TextAttack with Imperceptible Perturbations
University of Cambridge · 2024 – 2025

I also explored robustness-enhancing strategies for a DistilBERT emotion classifier, testing performance under both one-shot and differential evolution-style attacks. The work was supervised by Nicholas Boucher and Alastair Beresford.
